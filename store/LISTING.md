# Crossweave — store listing

**Category:** Puzzle
**Type:** Standalone (no backend; progress is saved in the browser)

**One-liner:** Swipe letters from a wheel to fill a crossword: 200 levels and a daily puzzle. Free, no tracking.

## Description

Crossweave turns a wheel of letters into a crossword. Swipe through the letters to spell a word. If it fits the grid, its letters appear in the right squares. Any other real word you make goes into the bonus jar, and every three bonus words earn a hint that reveals one letter.

- 200 levels in eight packs, with wheels growing from four to seven letters
- A new daily puzzle every UTC day, timed, with a leaderboard
- Shuffle the wheel whenever you like
- Your progress and bonus words are saved in your browser
- Works offline once loaded, in light and dark themes
- Free forever, MIT-licensed, with no ads, accounts or tracking
